## How to read it for this course

- **S09, Checkpoint: the minimalist's challenge.** R1, the README and core loop of mini-SWE-agent. Then P5, AI Agents That Matter (abstract, and the sections on cost-controlled evaluation and on overfitting to benchmarks). It is read here, not in M7, because every measure step from now on needs its argument.
- **S31, Cost matters.** Re-read P5's cost-controlled evaluation section (first read in S09). Optional: P27, Stop Comparing LLM Agents Without Disclosing the Harness, a position paper arguing that harness configuration explains more variance than model choice. Decide whether your history.tsv supports it.
